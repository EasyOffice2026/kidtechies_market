# Audience

Three people. Gulf-first, English-language, parent-paid. Use these for
persona-specific drafting - **not** as demographic ad-targeting parameters
invented beyond what is written here.

## Primary - the Vision 2030 parent (~60% of effort)

Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Doha. Professional, 32-45, one to three children in
private or international school. Their child's school has started teaching AI -
Saudi rolled a national AI curriculum into public education from the 2025-26
year, and the UAE has made AI compulsory from kindergarten. They feel a gap they
cannot fill themselves.

**In their own words:**
> "The school sent a letter about AI classes. I don't know enough to help him
> with it."

**What they need to hear first:** that catching up is possible, quickly, without
becoming technical. Message rank 1 - understanding, not just using.

**What loses them:** jargon, condescension, anything implying they are already
behind as a parent.

## Secondary - the worried screen-time parent (~25%)

Global English-speaking. Buys on safety before curriculum. Already anxious about
chatbots, deepfakes and what their child shares online. "Smart and Safe AI
Heroes" is the course they buy first, then they backfill the rest.

**In their own words:**
> "I'd rather she learns how it fools people from you than from TikTok."

**What they need to hear first:** the privacy architecture, and that the course
teaches spotting manipulation. Message ranks 2 and 1.

**What loses them:** any hint that we amplify the anxiety to sell. This persona
is *already* frightened; the brand's job is to be the calm one in the room.
Fear-based copy aimed here is both prohibited and counterproductive.

## Multiplier - the teacher and the homeschool lead (~15%)

Private-school ICT coordinators, after-school club leads, homeschool co-op
organisers. They need no login, they can project a mission on a screen, and
printable sheets solve their offline problem. One of them reaches 30 families at
once.

**In their own words:**
> "Is there anything I can run in a 40-minute club with no devices for half the
> room?"

**What they need to hear first:** no student accounts, no data collected,
classroom-ready, printables for the half of the room without a device. This is
also exactly what a school data-protection officer needs to hear.

**Treat every teacher as a channel, not a customer.**
