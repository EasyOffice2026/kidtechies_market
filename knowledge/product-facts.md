## How to use the runtime figure

`23:48` is **not a headline benefit** and must never appear next to the price as
the primary value statement. Read literally it says $2.94 per minute, against
competitors at $11-25 per month, and a comparing parent bounces on that line
alone (dossier S01, Blocker 02).

- **Lead with:** 8-9 hours of guided learning, 14 missions, 28 activities,
  70 quiz questions, 4 capstone projects, printable certificate.
- **Runtime is a craft detail, placed lower:** "every second animated by hand -
  no filler lectures."
